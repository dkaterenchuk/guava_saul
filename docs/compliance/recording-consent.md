# Recording consent and AI disclosure

> **Status: DRAFT. Needs counsel review.**

- Some states require **all-party consent** to record a call (e.g., California). The safe default is to treat every call as all-party consent: announce the recording up front, and continue only once the caller agrees or stays on the line, depending on what counsel approves.
- **AI disclosure:** state that the caller is talking to an AI in the first turn (principle S1). Several states have, or are considering, bot-disclosure laws.
- If the caller declines recording, decide the behavior here: either end the call with a referral, or continue without recording (only if we can actually guarantee that).

## Opening script (draft)
> "Hi, you've reached [name]. I'm an AI assistant, not a lawyer. This call is recorded to help with your request. I can explain legal information and connect you with help. What's going on?"
