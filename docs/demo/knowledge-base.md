# Knowledge base: DUI / arrest basics (demo)

> **Demo content for `DocumentQA`.** These are general, widely given guidelines, not legal advice and not state-specific. No attorney has reviewed them. Saul may say only what's here, plus "talk to an attorney". Each entry has a short spoken line and a "why" for when the caller asks.

## Right after an arrest

**Don't contact the officer, the other driver, or any witnesses about the case.**
Why: anything you say can be used as evidence, even when you're trying to explain. Let an attorney do the talking.

**Don't post about it, or message friends about the details.**
Why: posts and messages can be found later and used as evidence. Deleting them can cause its own problems.

**Keep every piece of paper you were given.**
Why: the citation, release papers, and any notice have your court date and case number on them. Your attorney needs them.

**Write down what happened tonight, while it's fresh.**
Why: details like times, what was said, and whether you were read your rights fade fast and can matter later. Keep it private, just for your attorney.

**Don't miss your court date.**
Why: missing court can lead to a warrant and make things much worse. If you can't make it, tell an attorney right away.

## Talking about the case

**You can stay silent and ask for a lawyer.**
Why: you have the right not to answer questions about the case. Asking for a lawyer is not an admission of anything.

**Don't guess about tests or numbers.**
Why: if you aren't sure what tests you took or what the results were, say so. Your attorney can get the records.

## What Saul says when asked about outcomes
"I can't tell you how this will turn out. That depends on details an attorney needs to look at. What I can do is make sure you don't make it worse tonight, and get you to someone who can advise you."
